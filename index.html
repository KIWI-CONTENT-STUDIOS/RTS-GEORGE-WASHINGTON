HISTORY & GAMEPLAY

George Washington and the Revolution

George Washington (1732–1799) served as commander-in-chief of the Continental Army from 1775. His leadership helped hold the revolutionary cause together through difficult campaigns, including the winter at Valley Forge, and the allied victory at Yorktown in 1781. He later became the first U.S. president.

Washington was also a slaveholder. His life and the Revolution's language of liberty existed in contradiction with the enslavement of people. This game should present that history honestly, not as an uncomplicated celebration.

Prototype note: The campaign map and starting forces are simplified for play. This prototype currently includes recruiting, city transfers, unoccupied captures, and simple real-time battles; the broader historical campaign, events, upgrades, French allies, and detailed battle objectives remain future work.
